# reports/paper_live/

Generated by `python3 -m paper_engine.run_paper_live --report`.

Two kinds of file live here and neither is committed:

- `journal.jsonl` — every paper decision, accepted **and refused**, hash-chained.
  Refusals are the point: after thirty days the question worth answering is how
  often the rule fired and was stopped by cost, latency or risk, rather than by
  the absence of a signal.
- `YYYY-MM-DD.md` — the day's report: signals, accepts, refusals with their
  reasons, mean gross and cost estimates, and the paper book if one exists.

They stay out of git because they are regenerable and they grow daily. The
directory is tracked so the engine has somewhere to write on a fresh clone.

Today the engine has nothing to trade: no mechanism holds a `PAPER_ELIGIBLE`
verdict, so the report says so and the journal stays empty. That is the correct
state, not a fault.
